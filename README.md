# BB Youth Financial Companion · concept prototype

Navigable HTML prototype developed in the **InovaXcelerate** program (FGV × TUM International) for the Banco do Brasil challenge: *how can BB use AI to deliver a more personalized, proactive and frictionless digital experience for young customers?*

> **Disclaimer.** This is a concept prototype for internal discussion and usability testing. It is **not** an official Banco do Brasil product or app. All screens, amounts, card numbers, limits and rates are fictitious and illustrative. No data is collected or sent anywhere: everything runs locally in the browser.

## What is inside

- **Prototype**: a phone mock-up with 14 navigable screens (onboarding by life stage, home with proactive insight, AI chat, goals, spending limits, credit card, card studio, financial health score, first investment simulator, offers with "why am I seeing this", monthly review, family mode, help and privacy). A side panel explains the rationale, evidence, hypothesis and pilot KPIs behind each screen.
- **Journey**: the three-stage journey (first contact, engagement, life companion).
- **Rationale & evidence**: value proposition, survey findings (N=83) and what was adapted from the base prototype.
- **Validation plan**: usability test script, hypotheses and decision criteria, Innovation Sprint implementation plan.

### Card studio

Users personalize the digital credit card with a live preview:
- **Look**: color (including BB yellow), pattern, horizontal or vertical orientation, sticker
- **Text**: card nickname and printed name
- **Rules**: personal spending cap below the approved limit, online, international and contactless toggles, auto-pay in full, round-ups to a savings goal

## Run it

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. No build step, no dependencies (fonts load from Google Fonts when online).

To publish with **GitHub Pages**: *Settings → Pages → Build and deployment → Deploy from a branch → `main` / root*.
